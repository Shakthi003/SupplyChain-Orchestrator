SUPPLIER CONTRACT — Global Parts Ltd (SUP-TEST-01)

AGREEMENT DATE: January 15, 2024
CONTRACT REFERENCE: GPC-2024-001
BUYER: SupplyChain Orchestrator Corp
SUPPLIER: Global Parts Ltd

---

## 1. PARTIES AND SCOPE

This agreement is entered into between SupplyChain Orchestrator Corp ("Buyer") and
Global Parts Ltd ("Supplier"). The Supplier agrees to supply hydraulic components,
electronic control units, and precision fasteners as listed in Schedule A.

---

## 2. DELIVERY TERMS

Standard lead time: 14 days from order confirmation date.
Emergency lead time: 7 days with prior written approval from the Buyer.
All delivery dates are confirmed at the time of purchase order issuance.
The Supplier must notify the Buyer within 24 hours of any anticipated delay.
Delivery location: Buyer's primary warehouse WH-01, Chicago, Illinois.

---

## 3. PENALTY CLAUSES

Delays beyond the confirmed delivery date will incur the following penalties,
deducted automatically from the corresponding invoice:

- Delay of 1 to 3 days: 2% of the total purchase order value
- Delay of 4 to 7 days: 5% of the total purchase order value
- Delay of 8 to 14 days: 10% of the total purchase order value
- Delay exceeding 14 days: 15% of the total purchase order value, plus the Buyer
  reserves the right to source from an alternate supplier at the Supplier's expense

Force majeure events (natural disasters, port closures, government embargoes)
are exempt from penalty clauses provided written evidence is submitted within
48 hours of the disruption.

---

## 4. PAYMENT TERMS

Standard payment: Net 30 days from confirmed delivery date.
Early payment discount: 2% if settled within 10 days of delivery.
Late payment by Buyer: 1.5% interest per month on outstanding balance.
All invoices must reference the Purchase Order number.

---

## 5. QUALITY AND INSPECTION

The Buyer reserves the right to inspect all deliveries within 5 business days.
Defective units must be replaced within 10 business days at no additional cost.
Acceptable defect rate: less than 0.5% of units per shipment.
Shipments exceeding the defect threshold may be rejected in full.

---

## 6. MINIMUM ORDER QUANTITIES AND PRICING

Hydraulic Valves (PRT-104 equivalent): minimum order 50 units, $42.00 per unit
Electronic Control Units (PRT-112 equivalent): minimum order 20 units, $125.00 per unit
Precision Fastener Sets: minimum order 200 units, $8.50 per unit

Pricing is fixed for the duration of this contract (12 months).
Price renegotiation is permitted at 90-day notice prior to contract renewal.

---

## 7. ALTERNATE SOURCING RIGHTS

If the Supplier fails to deliver within 7 days of the revised ETA, the Buyer may:
- Source the required parts from any alternate supplier
- Charge the cost difference to the original Supplier
- Suspend future purchase orders pending a performance review

The Supplier's reliability score will be updated based on on-time delivery history.
Scores below 0.75 (75% on-time rate) will trigger a formal supplier review.

---

## 8. LIABILITY

Total liability of either party is limited to the value of the affected purchase order.
Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.
This clause does not limit liability for fraud or wilful misconduct.

---

## 9. TERM AND RENEWAL

This contract is valid for 12 months from the agreement date.
Automatic renewal applies unless either party gives 60 days written notice.
Either party may terminate for cause with 30 days written notice.

---

## 10. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, United States.
Disputes will be resolved by binding arbitration in Chicago, Illinois.
